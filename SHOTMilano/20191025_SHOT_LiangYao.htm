Now, in Everyday Technologies #shotmilano, Liang Yao is leading us on a journey in the development of soft drinks consumption and distribution in China, from home made sodas, to soda fountains, to imitation brand drinks
In the 1940s quality of local sodas led to disease outbreaks, to which the republican government responded with an increase in sanitary controls about the quality of production processes #shotmilano
These campaigns had however littles success: anyone who in the 1940s wanted to sell cheap imitation sodas could do so. There were not really ways to control them all. Here as well it became a class issue: who was well off could always recur to safe branded drinks #shotmilano
The brunt of soft drink transmitted epidemics was thus taken by the lower classes, unable to pay for branded products and left to the hygienic failures of an uncontrolled market #shotmilano
