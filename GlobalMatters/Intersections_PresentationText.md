**Intersections: Cultivating Global Histories of Science, Technology, and Environments**

This virtual seminar is the product of a cooperation between [Department 3 "Artifacts, Action, Knowledge"](https://www.mpiwg-berlin.mpg.de/dept-three) at MPIWG and the [research project "A Global History of Technology (GLOBAL-HoT)"](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/global-hot/the_project_global_hot/index.de.jsp) at TU Darmstadt.

Its aim is to seed a discussion bringing together diverse points of view about the ways in which global histories of science, technology, and environments can be produced. Three sessions have already been planned, each dedicated to a concept the coordination group thinks may help to (re)consider the place of the global in our disciplines:

- Session I: Landscape (Jan. 21, 2021, 14:00–15:00 CET), convened by David Drengk & Chun Xu
- Session II: Practice (Feb. 18, 2021, 14:00–15:00 CET), convened by Alejandra Osorio & Wilko Hardenberg
- Session III: Time and temporality (Mar. 18, 2021, 14:00–15:00 CET), convened by  Jonas van der Straeten, & Tamar Novick

Sessions are structured as open discussions, in which each participant is invited to bring their own expertise and suggestions for further readings. As a discussion starter the conveners will offer a set of  questions framing the issue at stake and a brief text presenting recent developments in the literature. All additional readings are to be considered absolutely voluntary and the choice of which questions to actually discuss will fall collectively on all participants.
