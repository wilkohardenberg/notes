"ecological knowledge has also been constructed _through_ space. Observing ecological phenomena happened largely _in situ_ through the embodied presence of scientists in the field and through their practical engagement with its spatial characteristics."

Spaces are entangled both materially and simbolically in the research practices of the ecologist. It is throughtheir engagement with particular spaces that ecologists make some features of the natrual world visible and accountableas scientific phenomena, whil at the same time, keeping others out of sight."
