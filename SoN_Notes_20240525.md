Similarities between recording sound of nature and the putting in writing of folklore: both freeze experience and make them ahistorical 

Modern audio recordings allow to produce some incredible effects (see David Monacchi), but we don't have anything like it for the past. Even if human words are unable to fully describe both soundscapes (Pecere, 129) and the emotions they activate, they are our only chance to imagine past soundscapes.
