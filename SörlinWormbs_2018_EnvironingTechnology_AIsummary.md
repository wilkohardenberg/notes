Summary of the main points from the article "Environing technologies: a theory of making environment" by Sverker Sörlin and Nina Wormbs:

Key Arguments:

1. Central Concept:
- "Environing technologies" shape how nature becomes environment
- These are processes through which humans impact and understand nature
- Technologies should be understood broadly as both material and conceptual practices

2. Three Main Types of Environing Technologies:
- Writing (documentation, reports, assessments)
- Sensing (monitoring, measuring, observing)
- Shaping (physical alterations and modifications)

3. Key Examples Discussed:
- Satellite technology and remote sensing
- Climate science and modeling
- Ecosystem services valuation
- Environmental assessments

4. Core Characteristics:
- Often unintentional in their environing effects
- Combine material and conceptual changes
- Work across multiple scales
- Create new ways of understanding human-environment relationships

5. Historical Context:
- Shifts from seeing environment as external to understanding it as co-produced
- Evolution from local to global understanding of environmental change
- Movement from additive to systemic views of environmental impacts

6. Significance:
- Helps explain how environments are both materially and conceptually constructed
- Provides framework for understanding human-environment relationships
- Important for addressing current environmental challenges
- Offers new perspective on environmental history

7. Implications:
- Need to reconsider how we understand environmental change
- Important for future environmental transformations
- Suggests new ways of thinking about technology and environment together
- Relevant for policy and practice
