Alain Corbin argues in _The Lure of the Sea_ the perception of the ocean before the 18th century as causing fear and repulsion, while alon the coasts there were only ruins (1995, 4). The sea was depicted by Corbin as a void space, an uninhabited alterity in respect to the land. It was only thorugh the invention of leisure that the sea was reframed as a space of pleasure (Cusacck, 2012). On the other hand the sea remained a space of "exploration and appropriation".[^2]

[^2]: "The idea of the ocean as a "free space" has recently begun to break down, with competing claims over its use (Steinberg 2001:208). At the same time, there are currently efforts to conserve parts of the ocean as an "ocean wilderness" (Kroll 2008, 1)""
