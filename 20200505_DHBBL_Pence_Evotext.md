What can Evotext do?

- the potential of simple analysis is underrated
- Craig-Zeta algorithm: finds marker words that distinguish papers in corpus A from those in sub-corpus B
- Cooccurence: what kind of words occure (significantly often) within a given distance of our word of interest
- all data can be outputted in spreadsheets (csv/txt format) (in accordance with copyright agreements)

They document everything: file formats, schemas, data processing: http://data.evotext.org
