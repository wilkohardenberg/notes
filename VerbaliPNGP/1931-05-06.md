Important figures came to the park for the hunts

Just to mention the personalities that obtained hunting permits on May 6, 1931

Enrico Paolo Salem, later _podestà_ of Trieste, http://www.istitutogasparini.it/prefazioni/salem.pdf (1 ibex)

Wilhelm von Opel, of the eponymous car-manifature (2 ibex)

Ernesto and Napoleone Rossi di Montelera, counts, Martini e Rossi (5 chamois each)

Giacomo Medici del Vascello, marquess, MP, later undesecretary in the Mussolini government (1 ibex and 4 chamois)
