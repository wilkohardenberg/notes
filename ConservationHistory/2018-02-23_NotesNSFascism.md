Welfare policies and institutions existed before the welfare state was set up (think of INPS in Italy and pension schemes in Bismarck Germany)

In the same way nature policies and environmental institution existed before the nature state. Indeed they contributed forming it providing state agencies the scope and power on which their joint autonomy within the state structure was later based. to reach the critical mass needed to become a sector of the state conservation proceded collating existing agencies and influencing the state through lobbying practices close to the core of the liberal state. Ironically this process reached its maximum of expression not under the clientelar liberal regime, which would have seemed much more apt to support a high bourgeois set of policies and develop a coherent set of agencies and policies, but under the violent and centralistic fascist regime. This was due mainly to reasons of propaganda, and indeed for at least 10 years after the march on Rome nature conservation was managed by actors and following models that showed great continuity wit the previous regime. Only with the whole system's decided turn towards totalitarianism ahead of the conquest of Ethiopia there was a radical attempt at fascistizing also nature conservation. With dreadful consequences.

The fascist state was an "imperfect machine", as claims Melis. Similarly imperfect where the states within the state, including the nature state.
